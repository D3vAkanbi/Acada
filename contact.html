FAQ

Common questions

How is plagiarism checked?

Every completed document is run through a similarity checker before delivery, along with an AI-content scan, and revised until it's below 15%.

How fast can you deliver?

Timelines depend on scope and complexity. Share your deadline in the form and we'll confirm a realistic delivery date up front before you commit to anything.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes, revisions are included until the work matches your requirements and your supervisor's feedback.

What subjects or fields do you cover?

From social sciences and humanities to STEM and business, our writers are matched to your specific discipline rather than assigned at random.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on scope, level of study, and deadline. Share your project details and we'll send a transparent quote with no hidden fees.

Can I communicate directly with my writer?

Yes. Once your project is assigned, you can share feedback and ask questions throughout the process to keep your work on track.

Is my personal information kept confidential?

Always. Your details and project content are never shared, resold, or reused for any other client.

What if I need urgent changes after delivery?

Reach out as soon as possible. Most of post-delivery revisions are turned around quickly, especially if your defense or submission date is close.

Do you work with students outside Nigeria?

Yes, we support students across Africa and beyond. Communication happens by email, WhatsApp, or phone regardless of your location.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer and other common local payment options. Full payment details are shared once your quote is confirmed.
